## What it is

DzGraph draws charts in a page and lets people explore them: point at a value, zoom into a month, compare two
series from the same start, select points with a lasso, click a year to see its months. It is written from scratch
in TypeScript, in layers you can take separately:

- **`@dzgraph/core`** — options and data in, a scene out. No DOM, no clock, no dependency: it runs in a browser, a
  worker or a server.
- **`@dzgraph/dom`** — draws the scene in SVG, on a Canvas or with WebGL2, and handles the pointer and the keyboard.
  Each series type and each feature is its own import: a page pays only for what it uses.
- **`@dzgraph/theme`** — the look, as CSS variables: light and dark, contrast checked.
- **`@dzgraph/angular`**, **`@dzgraph/dztooltip`**, **`@dzgraph/dzgrid`** — the chart in Angular, its tooltip drawn by
  DzTooltip, and a chart linked to a DzGrid grid.

The chart below is a real one, drawn by the library on this page. Point at it, press Tab then the arrow keys, drag
across the plot to zoom, click a legend item; switch the site to dark and the chart follows.

## Designed in, not added

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Explore" icon="magnifier">
    Zoom and a navigator, a crosshair, comparison from a point, measures, point selection (an exact lasso on a
    million points), aggregation by period, drilldown, linked charts — and the user's view as JSON.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Accessible" icon="approve-check">
    Every point reachable from the keyboard and named for screen readers, a data table equivalent to the chart, colour
    never alone (each series has its marker), contrast checked, reduced motion respected.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Any language, any direction" icon="translate">
    Numbers and dates by the locale, time in any zone, English and French words built in, right to left designed in.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Big data" icon="rocket">
    Typed columns, decimation that keeps a line's look, Canvas past a few thousand marks and WebGL2 for scatters of
    millions of points.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
